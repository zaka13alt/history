Getting started

Joining Ashcobe, step by step.

No long forms, no waiting rooms. Most students go from first visit to first lab in a few weeks.

i.

Choose a programme

Browse the four schools and find the pathway that suits what you want to build.

ii.

Tour the labs

Walk through the workshops and studios with a current student, and ask anything.

iii.

Have a chat

A short, informal conversation with a tutor about your interests and goals.

iv.

Start term

Pick your modules and begin with the shared foundation term.
